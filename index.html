Different kinds of sources. Different reasons for creating them.
WHEN YOU FIND A SOURCE ON GOOGLE OR THE OPEN WEB

Ask these three questions.

  1. Who created it?
  2. Where’s the evidence?
  3. What’s the purpose?
Library databases are selected pools of content; Google brings together research, news, social experience, ads, commerce, and AI features. Neither is simply good or bad. Ask why each item was created. On the final click, use the three questions to evaluate a source found through Google or on the open web.

Reproduced by Institute of the Black World 21st Century on 3 Oct. 2023: https://ibw21.org/editors-choice/kendi-raised-millions-with-promises-to-conquer-racism-what-went-wrong/ . Find “$12.3 million.” The journalists reviewed financial records for the spending number and attributed approximately $47 million remaining to BU. These figures have different accounting contexts and are not intended as a simple subtraction exercise. The reporting preceded the Oct. 24 attack. The Globe article also documents serious management complaints. Acknowledge these rather than treating all criticism as misinformation. Second arrow: BU's audit announcement and the narrowly stated conclusion. Doug Most, “Audit Finds No Issues, Concerns with Finances at Center for Antiracist Research,” BU Today, 7 Nov. 2023: https://www.bu.edu/articles/2023/audit-finds-no-issues-at-center-for-antiracist-research/ . Audit covered June 2020–September 2023 and examined appropriate charging of expenditures to grant and gift accounts. It was an internal university audit, not a finding that every aspect of the center's management was sound. BU had an institutional stake and was still reviewing workplace climate and grant reporting. Explain why an identified evidence source, reviewable methods, and corroboration matter even when a source has interests. Model note: “Financial reporting contradicts the claim that the center spent $43 million: $12.3 million spent in its first three years (Damiano and Burns).” Ask: Would a search result alone have revealed this?
